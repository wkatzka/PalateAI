Palate picks restaurants for you, puts them on a map and talks through the choice with you: what to order, when to go, who it suits.

In private beta on iPhone, in New York City. Coming to the App Store.

The art of taste, down to a science

Palate runs on its own restaurant database, layered over a culinary knowledge graph that links every dish, technique, ingredient and chef to the kitchens that share them.

Loved the dry-aged duck at a tasting counter? Palate follows the threads: the aging to a steakhouse that dry-ages in-house, the fermented glaze to a Korean kitchen making its own doenjang, the chef to the new place their former sous-chef just opened. Bespoke matching a chatbot can't fake.
